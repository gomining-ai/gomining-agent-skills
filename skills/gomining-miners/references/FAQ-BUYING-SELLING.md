# Buying and Selling

### How much does 1 TH cost?

The price per TH depends on where and how you buy:

**Primary marketplace (GoMining directly):**
The price per TH is set by GoMining and changes over time based on market conditions. The more TH you buy in a single purchase, the lower the price per TH — larger purchases get a better rate. You can see the exact current price in the app using the Create Miner calculator.

**Secondary marketplace (from other users):**
The price is set by the seller. Each listing has its own price per TH, which may be higher or lower than the primary marketplace rate. You can filter and compare listings to find the best deal.

### Where can I buy digital miners?

We recommend purchasing digital miners through your GoMining personal account.

**Why is it better to buy miners on our marketplace?**

* You don't have to worry about their authenticity.
* They will automatically appear in your personal account and start earning you daily BTC rewards right after purchase.
* You get immediate customer support that guides you through the whole process.

### How can I buy miners?

You can buy a miner in your personal account by creating your own miner or by purchasing on our Marketplace (available only for those who already have at least 1 miner).

#### Create miner

1. Log in to your GoMining personal account.
2. Go to **My miners.**
3. Click **Create miner**.
4. Choose the power and energy efficiency of your future digital miner
5. Pay with any payment method you like: by crypto or fiat. The list of payment methods varies by region.
6. Done! You'll receive your first BTC reward in just 24 hours

For in-app purchases via Apple, Google, and card: We need up to 90 days to verify your purchase. Until then, you'll not be able to list your miner for sale or withdraw (mint) it from your virtual wallet to another crypto wallet.

#### Buy on the marketplace

Available only for those who already have at least 1 miner.

1. Log in to your GoMining personal account, go to **Marketplace**
2. Choose any miner you like and click **Buy**
3. Select the receiving wallet address. Options:
   - **Ethereum wallet** — only for miners issued on the Ethereum network
   - **BSC wallet** — only for miners issued on the BSC network
   - **TON wallet** — only for miners issued on the TON network
   - **SOLANA wallet** — only for miners issued on the SOLANA network
   - **GoMining Virtual wallet** — suitable for **all** miners; from here you can send miners to an external wallet at any time
   (If needed, you can add the matching wallet during the purchase.)
4. Click **Pay** — you can pay only with GOMINING tokens (ERC-20, BEP-20, TON, and Virtual). Clicking Pay reserves the miner for **1 hour** to complete payment.
5. Select payment network — it's fine if the payment network/wallet differs from the receiving one.
6. Send the exact total amount to the indicated address.

Your miners are then stored in your receiving wallet and appear in your account under **Mining farm**; rewards appear in the **Rewards** section.

### What are "Hot deals" and "Auction" in the Marketplace?

They are **filter chips** at the top of the Marketplace **Miners** list:

- **Hot deals** — hot/featured offers: miners currently listed at especially attractive prices.
- **Auction** — miners that are currently listed **on auction** (i.e. via the step-down / auction pricing mode) rather than a plain fixed price.

The Marketplace also has **collection filter chips** (e.g. a specific collection name like "Jacob") to narrow the list to that collection. These are real, current Marketplace sections — don't tell users you have no information about "Hot deals" or "Auction".

### How do I find miners with a specific (low) energy efficiency or from a specific collection?

Use the Marketplace **Filters**. On **Marketplace → Miners**, open the **filter icon** (top-right of the Miners list, next to *Sell miner*). The Filters panel lets you narrow by: **Availability** (On sale), **Price**, **Price type**, **Energy efficiency** (enter a **min / max W/TH** range), **Power** (TH), and **Collections**. Set the Energy efficiency range to the W/TH you want and/or pick a collection, then **Apply**.

So when a user asks *"which collections have single-digit / low W/TH?"* or wants a specific efficiency, don't try to recite a collection-by-collection efficiency table (that isn't documented and changes) — point them to the **Energy efficiency filter** (and the Collections filter) so they can find exactly what's currently listed at their target W/TH.

### Can I set an alert / get notified when a specific miner or collection is listed on the Marketplace?

No. The Marketplace does **not** have a listing-alert, watchlist, or "notify me when available" feature. There is no way to subscribe to a specific miner or collection (e.g. a limited-edition **Greedy Machine**) and be pinged when one is listed.

Because secondary-market listings are created by other users and change continuously, the way to catch one is to **check the Marketplace periodically** and buy when it appears — availability can't be predicted or reserved in advance. You can use the Marketplace filters (by collection, currency, price) to find matching listings quickly each time you look.

### What have miners with power X and efficiency Y sold for in the past? (historical Marketplace sale prices)

**The assistant does not have a tool for historical Marketplace sale prices.** Your data tools return **current live listings** (what's on sale right now), not the sale history of comparable miners. You cannot produce a "past sale price for a 600 TH / 22 W/TH miner" list or an average sold-price statistic — no such tool call exists.

When the user asks about **past sale prices** for miners with specific power / efficiency / collection filters:

- Give the **current live benchmark** using the tool you do have (current Marketplace listings matching those specs) and label it clearly as "current listings, not sold history".
- Point the user to the two in-app places where **their own** transaction history lives: **Purchase history** (Mining Farm → clock icon) shows what they paid for their own miners; **Wallet → Recent transactions → All transactions → Miner purchase filter** shows the same. Neither shows other users' historical sale prices — only theirs.
- For a broader read on where a collection has traded historically, the **GoMining Discord** and community channels are where players discuss recent trades — mention it as a community-not-official source (per the Miner Wars community-channel exception in the system prompt) with the standard scam caution.
- Do **not** invent a "sold for approximately $X" figure or a "historical average" — those are not available. Do **not** promise to look it up "again later" — the tool won't exist next turn either.

State the limit once, offer the live benchmark, and stop — don't loop on "let me try another way."

### How to pay in installments?

#### What is Mine Now, Pay Later?

**Mine Now, Pay Later (MNPL)** lets you get a miner right away and pay for it over time. Instead of paying the full price upfront, you make a small first payment, and the rest is charged in equal monthly installments.

> **MNPL is only for buying a miner — not for upgrades.** MNPL applies to the **purchase of a miner**; it is **not** a payment option for **upgrading** an existing miner's power or efficiency. So if a user asks "which upgrades qualify for MNPL?", the answer is that **upgrades can't be paid via MNPL at all** — MNPL is purchase-only. Upgrades are paid from the payment-asset selector in the Upgrade flow (crypto from the wallet, incl. BTC, or card/Apple Pay). Don't imply an upgrade can be financed through MNPL.

Your miner starts mining at full power immediately.

The plan includes:
* Only the installment fee
* Fixed monthly payments (charged every 30 days)
* No interest or hidden charges

#### Who can use Mine Now, Pay Later?

Any registered user can choose this payment method if it's available in their region. If a single payment or installment exceeds $1,000, you'll be asked to complete KYC before continuing.

#### How do MNPL payments work?

MNPL splits the miner's price into a 25% upfront payment and equal monthly installments. Payments are charged automatically every 30 days.

The number of monthly installments for a purchase is shown at checkout (it follows from the miner price and the 25% down payment) — there is no single fixed maximum count. You can also have **multiple active MNPL plans running at the same time**; you don't need to finish one before starting another.

#### Are there any restrictions until I finish all payments?

Yes. While the plan is active, you can't:

* Sell the miner
* Mint the miner
* Upgrade the miner (including subscription upgrades and reward reinvestment)

Once the plan is fully paid, these restrictions are removed. Upgrades become available immediately, while minting and selling unlock after 90 days.

#### Can my miner be confiscated?

Yes, but only if your MNPL payment is more than 27 days overdue.

Before that:
* You'll receive reminders.
* Mining pauses after **7 days** of non-payment.
* You can still restore the plan by paying the missed installment.

If confiscated, the miner is listed on the Marketplace and you receive a refund of your paid installments minus earned rewards, payment provider fees, and installation fees.

#### What does MNPL qualify for?

MNPL purchases qualify for most GoMining programs:

| Program | MNPL Qualification |
|---------|-------------------|
| Raffle tickets | 1 ticket per successful installment payment |
| Referral program | Qualifies (referrer receives bonus; buyer referral bonuses TBD) |
| Bounty program | Qualifies same as regular purchases |
| Ambassador program | Qualifies same as regular purchases |
| VIP program | All payments count toward VIP progress from day one |
| Bonus Miner task | Fully qualifies |

#### Can I use a promo code with MNPL?

No. Promo codes are not available for MNPL payments at this time.

#### What payment methods can I use for MNPL installments?

Two options are supported for MNPL installment payments:

1. **Bank card** (Visa / Mastercard)
2. **Crypto** from your GoMining wallet balance

**You cannot switch between card and crypto after the first installment.** Whichever branch you chose for the first payment (card **or** crypto) is locked in for **every** subsequent installment of that plan. There is no in-app toggle to switch a card-based plan to crypto or vice versa mid-plan.

If a user asks *"can I pay with GMT tokens?"* — that falls under the **crypto** branch (paid from your GoMining wallet); if the plan was set up on **card** at the first installment, it cannot switch to crypto (or GMT specifically) later. If the plan started on **crypto** and the user wants to change the specific asset within the crypto branch, that isn't documented as switchable either — treat any mid-plan payment-method change request as unsupported and escalate if the user is stuck.

#### Can I change my payment method for installments?

No. Once a payment method is selected during the first installment, it cannot be changed for subsequent payments. All future installments are charged using the same payment type (card or crypto — see the section above).

#### Why is MNPL not available to me?

MNPL might not be offered in your case for one of these reasons:

* **Region** — MNPL isn't available in all regions; if your country isn't supported, the option won't appear at checkout.
* **KYC for larger amounts** — if a single payment/installment exceeds **$1,000**, you must complete KYC first.
* **Recent default** — if you had an MNPL default in the **last 180 days**, a new plan isn't available until that window passes.
* **Miner eligibility** — the miner must be under **1000 TH**, have energy efficiency better than **12 W/TH**, and **not** be from the **Greedy Machines** collection.

If none of these seem to apply and the option is still missing or errors out, direct the user to GoMining support.

### What does minting a miner to my wallet do?

A Minebox miner created in the app starts in your **GoMining Wallet** as **unminted**. Minting assigns it to a blockchain network (**Ethereum**, **BSC**, etc.) and issues it as an on-network asset in your own external wallet, which is what makes it **transferable and sellable** outside GoMining.

- You only need to mint if you plan to **sell on the Marketplace** or **move the miner to an external crypto wallet**. If you just want to keep mining, you don't have to mint.
- The network you pick affects future external transfers/fees only — **your mining rewards are unaffected**, and the miner keeps earning in your GoMining account after minting.
- The **Mint** button is in the menu behind the three dots next to the Upgrade button on the miner card (you'll see **Mint** instead of **Sell** until it's minted).
- Note: miners bought via Apple Pay / Google Pay / card or via MNPL can only be minted after the 90-day restriction (for MNPL, 90 days after the final payment).

### Where can I sell miners?

### Can I gift a digital miner to another user?

**You cannot transfer / gift an already-owned miner to someone else.** A miner you already have in your account cannot be sent to another user as a gift. The only way to move an owned miner to another person is to **sell** it (either via the GoMining Marketplace or on a public NFT marketplace after minting) — the other person then buys it. There is no "transfer to another account" flow for owned miners.

**gifts.gomining.com is for something different — buying a NEW miner AS a gift.** The `https://gifts.gomining.com/` site is a **purchase flow** where you buy a brand-new miner and have it delivered to a friend's GoMining account as a gift. It's a way to give someone a miner by paying for it yourself — it is **not** a way to transfer a miner you already own. If a user opens gifts.gomining.com expecting to see their own miners listed as "available to gift" and reports **"there's nothing to gift"** (or similar), that is the expected state — their owned miners will never appear there because that page doesn't handle transfers of existing miners; it's a purchase interface.

**How many times can a miner be gifted?** The question doesn't apply — since owned miners can't be transferred at all, there's no per-miner transfer count / limit / cooldown. A newly-purchased-as-gift miner belongs to the recipient like any other miner they own; they cannot then pass it on as a gift either (they can only sell it).

**Summary for the assistant:**
- User asks *"how can I gift my miner to X"* → answer: **cannot gift an existing miner**, only sell it (Marketplace). Explain gifts.gomining.com is a new-miner-purchase-as-gift flow, not a transfer.
- User asks *"how many times can a miner be gifted / transferred"* → answer: **no such transfer exists**, so the count question doesn't apply.
- User asks *"I opened gifts.gomining.com and there's nothing to gift"* → clarify: that's a new-miner-purchase flow, not a transfer of owned miners — that's why they see nothing of their own there.

This is distinct from the GoMining **Card "Gift Miner"** promo (a free 1 TH bonus miner tied to the card) — that's a different feature. Don't confuse the two.

### How can I see how much I paid for each miner (purchase price history)?

Yes — the price you paid for each miner is available in the app's **Purchase history**:

1. Open the **Mining farm** tab.
2. Scroll to the **Miners** section.
3. Click the **clock (history) icon** at the top-right of the Miners list.

The **Purchase history** window opens with two tabs:

* **Miners** — each row shows the date, miner (name + TH + W/TH), the **price paid**, and status (e.g. Success). Toggle **Show canceled purchases** to include cancelled ones.
* **Upgrades** — the same view for TH/efficiency upgrades.

Each row has two buttons: the first opens the GoMining Pay transaction receipt, and the second opens the **invoice**, which can be downloaded as a PDF (showing invoice number, date, line items, and net/tax/total).

**Alternative path — Wallet transaction history:** you can also see what you paid via **Wallet → Recent transactions → "All transactions"**, then in the **Transactions** filter under **Outgoing** tick **Miner purchase** (and/or **Power upgrade**, **Efficiency upgrade**, **Miner installment**) and **Apply**. Each matching row in **History** shows the date, type, and the amount paid. A **"You've purchased a digital miner!"** email confirmation is also sent at purchase.

> **Note:** opening an **individual miner's card** does **not** show its purchase price — don't send users there for it. The price paid lives in the **Purchase history** window (Mining farm → clock icon) or the **Wallet → All transactions → Miner purchase** filter above. The assistant's data tools do not expose purchase price directly, so point the user to one of these two in-app locations.
