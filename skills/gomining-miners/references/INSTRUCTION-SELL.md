# How to Sell Your Miner

## Before You Sell

Complete these steps for the miner you want to sell:

1. **Turn off TH reinvestment** — if this miner is the reinvestment target, disable it or assign another miner.
2. **Cancel active subscriptions** — cancel any active power upgrade subscriptions on this miner.
3. **Mint the miner (if needed)** — if your miner was created as a Minebox and is stored in your GoMining Wallet without being minted, you will see a **Mint** button instead of **Sell**. Minting assigns the miner to a blockchain network and makes it tradeable. Minting is a separate action from selling — for the full steps, see [How to Mint a Miner](INSTRUCTION-MINT.md).

---

## 90-Day Selling Restriction

Miners purchased or upgraded via **Apple Pay, Google Pay, or card** cannot be sold or withdrawn for **90 days** from the date of the most recent qualifying purchase or upgrade.

| Scenario | 90-day applies? |
|----------|----------------|
| Purchased via Apple/Google/card | Yes |
| Purchased via crypto | No |
| Upgraded via Apple/Google/card | Yes — timer resets |
| MNPL — final installment paid | Yes — 90 days from final payment |

- All timing is based on UTC.
- See GoMining Terms of Service, Section 2.2 for full details.

## How to Sell

### Step 1 — Find the Sell button

The **Mint** and **Sell** buttons are in the menu behind the three dots next to the Upgrade button on the miner card. The **Sell** button only appears if the miner is eligible for selling.

### Step 2 — Set your price

All Marketplace payments and prices use **GOMINING tokens**. Two pricing modes:

- **Fixed** — one set price. **Minimum listing price: 10 GOMINING.**
- **Step-down** — the price starts higher and automatically steps down over time by a chosen **step size** at a chosen **interval** (1, 4, or 24 hours). **Minimum starting price: 10.25 GOMINING.**

The **maximum listing price is 500,000 GOMINING** (the cap applies to both pricing modes). So a listing price must be between the minimum above and 500,000 GOMINING.

The app shows the current **market price** for miners with similar power and efficiency and warns you if your price is far from it.

**Marketplace fee: 5%.** It is **deducted from your sale proceeds** — your **"You'll get"** amount is the price minus the 5% fee (e.g. list at 100 GOMINING → you receive 95). The listing screen shows the exact breakdown (price per TH, 5% fee, and what you'll get) before you confirm.

> Double-check your price before confirming — once listed, the miner can be purchased instantly and the sale cannot be undone.

### Step 3 — Confirm the listing

**GoMining Wallet miners:** Tap Sell — the miner appears on the Marketplace immediately.

**BSC / Ethereum / TON / Solana miners:**
1. Connect the wallet on the same network as the miner.
2. Approve the smart contract (on BSC/ETH: permission for transfer; on TON/SOL: miner moves temporarily to the Marketplace wallet).
3. Ensure your wallet has enough BNB / ETH / TON / SOL for network fees.
4. Tap Sell.

## While Your Miner is Listed

- Keeps earning rewards (Mining Mode or Miner Wars) until sold.
- Cannot be upgraded, withdrawn, set for TH reinvestment, or have subscriptions added.
- To change the price: remove from sale, then relist at the new price.

## Cancelling / Removing a Listing

You can remove a listed miner from sale at any time before it's bought — the miner returns to your Mining Farm and is no longer visible on the Marketplace. Only the **5% Marketplace fee at sale** (deducted from the seller's proceeds when a listing sells) is documented. **Whether cancelling / removing a listing itself carries a fee is not documented in this skill.**

> When a user asks "is there a fee to cancel my listing?" or "what does it cost to unlist / delist?", do **not** state "no fee" from memory (that's an unverified inference) and do **not** guess a figure. Say plainly that this skill documents the 5% at-sale fee but does not document a separate cancellation fee, and offer to connect them with GoMining support if they need a definitive answer for their listing. Do not conflate the 5% sale fee with a cancellation fee — they are different questions.

## Secondary Market Miners

Miners purchased on the secondary market **do not count toward VIP progression** at time of purchase. However, any **upgrades** made to those miners via GoMining do count toward VIP.
