# Miner Collections

## Overview

Every digital miner in GoMining belongs to a collection, which determines its appearance, specs, and features. There are three main collection types: Bonus Miner, Limited-edition collections, and the Minebox collection.

---

## Bonus Miner

Every new user receives a free Bonus Miner automatically — no purchase needed.

### Purpose

The Bonus Miner is a learning tool designed to help new users explore GoMining, earn their first BTC, and get comfortable with the platform before making any purchases.

### How to Activate

1. Open the GoMining app and go to your Mining Farm.
2. Tap the Bonus Miner, then tap **Activate**.
3. This unlocks a list of onboarding tasks.

### How to Increase Power

Complete onboarding tasks to boost mining power and personalize the miner's appearance:
- The first task is already complete — just tap Activate to start.
- Complete other tasks in any order.
- Each task has a time limit — claim the power before it expires.

### Limitations

| Limitation | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Reward withdrawal | **Disabled** until the user creates their first paid miner |
| Bonus Miner power | Does not count toward VIP level |
| Marketplace | Cannot be sold or minted |
| Miner Wars | Bonus Miner power counts in-game, but you **cannot enter Miner Wars with only a Bonus Miner** — you need at least one paid miner |

Once you create your first paid miner, all rewards accumulated by the Bonus Miner become withdrawable.

## Limited-Edition Collections

Limited-edition miners are released occasionally and tied to special themes or events.

### Key Characteristics

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Availability | Only on the Marketplace; new drops sell out quickly |
| Visual design | Fixed — known before purchase, not randomly generated |
| Mining specs | Preset power and efficiency — cannot be customized |
| Blockchain network | Fixed at creation by GoMining — cannot be changed |

### Notable Example: Greedy Machines

The Greedy Machines collection (launched 2022) was the very first GoMining collection. Its standout feature: **mining power automatically increases every week**, making it unique among all collections. (Applies to Greedy Machines with an energy efficiency of 20 W/TH or lower.)

**There is no fixed weekly increase rate.** The weekly power boost for Greedy Machines is funded by the **10% GOMINING-rewards redistribution**, and how much goes to it each week is decided by the **weekly community reward-distribution vote** (alongside the Extra Mining-mode discount and the Miner Wars prize fund). So the amount **varies week to week** — there is no set TH/s or percentage to quote. Users can see their actual weekly increase in their **Mining Farm**.

### Important Notes

- Limited-edition miners cannot have their power or efficiency customized.
- They are already minted on a specific blockchain and cannot be moved to a different network.
- Their blockchain network only matters if you plan to transfer to an external wallet.

## Minebox Collection

Minebox is the standard collection available to all users directly in the app — it's the default choice for creating a new miner.

### Key Characteristics

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Availability | Directly in the GoMining app via "Create Miner" |
| Power (TH/s) | Fully customizable, up to 5000 TH/s. Minimum is **0.75 TH** in supported countries (0.1 TH upgrade step); it is no longer a fixed 1 TH floor. Availability of the 0.75 TH entry tier depends on the user's KYC-verified country. |
| Efficiency (W/TH) | Customizable, up to the best energy efficiency currently available on the platform |
| Visual design | **Randomly generated** after purchase from a wide range of traits |
| Blockchain network | Choose ETH or BSC when minting to an external wallet |

### How It Works

1. Select power (TH/s) and efficiency (W/TH) during creation.
2. Use the built-in calculator to estimate daily, monthly, and yearly BTC rewards.
3. After purchase, a unique visual design is randomly generated — the final look is revealed only after generation.
4. The miner mines immediately in Mining Mode.

### Minting (Optional)

Minebox miners created in the app start in your GoMining Wallet (unminted). You only need to mint if you plan to:
- Sell on the Marketplace
- Move to an external crypto wallet

When minting, you choose the blockchain network: **Ethereum** (higher decentralization, higher fees) or **BSC** (lower fees, faster transactions). This choice only affects future external transfers — rewards are unaffected.

### Upgrading

Minebox miners can be upgraded at any time after purchase:
- **Power:** up to 5000 TH/s
- **Efficiency:** up to the best energy efficiency currently available on the platform

## Collection Comparison

| Feature | Bonus Miner | Limited-Edition | Minebox |
|---------|------------|----------------|---------|
| Cost | Free | Marketplace (GOMINING tokens) | Paid (multiple methods) |
| Power customization | Via tasks only | Fixed | Fully customizable |
| Visual design | Task-based | Fixed (known before purchase) | Random (revealed after purchase) |
| Blockchain | N/A | Fixed by GoMining | Choose when minting (ETH or BSC) |
| VIP contribution | No | Yes (if primary sale) | Yes |
| Sellable | No | Yes (if minted) | Yes (after minting) |
| Upgradeable | No | Yes | Yes |
| Withdrawal restriction | Cannot withdraw rewards until first purchase | Standard rules | Standard rules |
