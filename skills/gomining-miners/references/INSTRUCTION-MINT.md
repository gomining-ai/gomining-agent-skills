# How to Mint a Miner

**Minting is not the same as creating or selling a miner.** Creating a miner (MineBox generation in **Digital Miners**) produces a miner stored in your GoMining Wallet. *Minting* is a separate, optional step that assigns a miner you already own to a blockchain network and makes it tradeable. Selling is yet another action — you only need to mint first if you want to sell an unminted GoMining Wallet miner on an external network.

## Before You Mint

- You can only mint a miner you already own.
- Minting requires KYC Basic.

## How to Mint

1. Set up a crypto wallet (MetaMask or any WalletConnect-compatible wallet). Save your seed phrase.
2. Add crypto to cover network fees:
   - ~$2 in BNB for BSC
   - ~$10 in ETH for Ethereum
3. Open the miner card, tap the three dots next to **Upgrade**, and choose **Mint**.
4. Select your preferred network, connect your wallet, and confirm the minting transaction.

Once minted, the miner appears in your wallet on the chosen network while continuing to earn rewards in your GoMining account.

> **Which wallet to connect:** minting connects via **MetaMask** or any **WalletConnect-compatible** wallet — those are the supported connection methods. If a user tries another wallet's app and the option "keeps redirecting to download" or won't connect, guide them to use **MetaMask** or the **WalletConnect** option instead (many wallets, e.g. OKX, can connect through WalletConnect) rather than the wallet's own direct button. Don't confirm support for a specific third-party wallet you can't verify; point them to MetaMask / WalletConnect, and escalate if it still won't connect.

> Miners purchased or upgraded via Apple Pay, Google Pay, or card cannot be minted or withdrawn for 90 days from the most recent qualifying purchase or upgrade. Miners paid with crypto are not subject to this restriction.
