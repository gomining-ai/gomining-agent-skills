# Technical Equipment

### What pools do you work with?

GoMining works with **Binance Pool**, **VIA BTC**, and **Foundry**. Users can pick which of these three their rewards are generated from in **Mining Farm → Settings → Pool** (mobile: **My Miners → Settings → Pool**); see the [Reward Settings section in FAQ-REWARDS](../../gomining-miners/references/FAQ-REWARDS.md) for the full settings panel. The Binance Pool link, for reference, is https://pool.binance.com/en.

---

### How are funds used to update the equipment fleet?

All funds received from staking and mining are reinvested into the development and expansion of our device fleet.
