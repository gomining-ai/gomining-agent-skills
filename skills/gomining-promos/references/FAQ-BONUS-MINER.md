# Bonus Miner

> Note: Bonus Miner types and terms may vary between users. The company may offer different versions of the feature as part of marketing tests or seasonal experiments.

## What is a bonus miner?

The Bonus Miner is a free promotional tool that lets you try GoMining and earn additional rewards. By completing different in-app tasks, you can increase its mining power and personalize its look.

---

## How long does the Bonus Miner last? Is there a trial period?

There is no trial period or expiry date — your Bonus Miner mines permanently. Every task you complete in the app adds hashrate to it, and more hashrate means more BTC every day.

Tasks unlock by level: some are available right away, while others open up after you buy a miner or upgrade its power or efficiency. New tasks appear regularly, so check back to see what's available.

> While the Bonus Miner itself never expires, withdrawing its rewards requires a qualifying purchase. See [Can I withdraw rewards from the Bonus Miner?](#can-i-withdraw-rewards-from-the-bonus-miner) below.

---

## Where can I find my Bonus Miner?

There are two ways to find your Bonus Miner:

- My Miners → Mining Farm: Your Bonus Miner is listed with your other miners in the Miners section.
- Promo Section: Open the Promos area and choose Bonus Miner tasks.

---

## How do I start using it?

Visit the Bonus Miner page and click Activate. After activation, start completing tasks to upgrade your miner.

---

## How does the task system work?

Tasks let you earn extra rewards and help you get the most out of your Bonus Miner.

1. Look for available tasks in the **Tasks** tab on the Bonus Miner page or in the **Promos** section.
2. Finish each task within its deadline. After you complete a task, you usually need to click **Claim** on the task card to receive your reward; only a few tasks are credited automatically.

> If you do not click **Claim** before the timer runs out, the task moves to `Expired`, and the reward is lost.

---

## When do new tasks appear?

There is **no fixed schedule** — new Bonus Miner tasks appear irregularly and can show up at any time (some also unlock as you buy a miner or upgrade its power/efficiency). There is no weekly, monthly, or event-based release cadence to predict. Check the **Tasks** tab on the Bonus Miner page (or the **Promos** section) regularly to see what's currently available.

**Do expired tasks get replaced by new ones?** There is **no guaranteed 1-for-1 replacement** of an expired task. Expired tasks are simply gone (their reward is lost), and new tasks appear on the same irregular, no-fixed-cadence basis described above — they are not issued specifically to "replace" the ones you missed. So the honest answer to "will new tasks replace the expired ones?" is: new tasks may appear over time, but not as direct replacements and not on any predictable schedule — keep checking the Tasks tab.

---

## Do I need to claim my task rewards?

Yes. For almost all tasks, the reward is **not** credited automatically.

Once you meet the requirements, a **Claim** button appears on the task card in your active tasks. Press **Claim** before the timer ends to receive your reward. If you miss the deadline, the task moves to `Expired`, and the reward is lost.

Only a few tasks are credited automatically.

---

## What types of tasks are available?

Most tasks encourage you to use different features of the service and help you get comfortable with how everything works.

Tasks are grouped by difficulty level. The tasks you see will depend on what you have already done in GoMining:

- Level 1: These tasks are available right away, with no extra steps needed.
- Level 2: These tasks become available after you buy a new miner.
- Level 3: These tasks unlock after you upgrade your miner's power or efficiency.

New tasks for your Bonus Miner can show up at any time, so check back regularly.

> GoMining reserves the right to modify or remove tasks before they are completed.

## How do I upgrade the power of my Bonus Miner?

You can make your Bonus Miner more powerful by earning special bonuses, which you usually get for finishing tasks.

You earn most power bonuses by completing tasks and then claiming them — press **Claim** on the task card to apply the upgrade.

To see all your current and available power upgrades, go to the **Upgrades** section on the Bonus Miner page.

### What is the maximum power of the Bonus Miner?

There is **no fixed maximum power cap** for the Bonus Miner and no published target figure. Its power simply **grows as you complete tasks** — each power task you claim adds hashrate — so how high it goes depends on how many tasks you complete, not on a set ceiling. Don't quote a specific maximum TH number (that would be invented); explain that it increases task-by-task with no stated cap, and that the current power and completed/available upgrades are shown on the Bonus Miner page (**Upgrades** tab).

## Can I withdraw rewards from the Bonus Miner?

You can only withdraw rewards from the Bonus Miner after you buy a miner of any power directly from GoMining or open a GoMining Card. This rule helps prevent misuse and keeps things fair for everyone.

> Buying a miner on the secondary market, receiving one in your crypto wallet, or obtaining one through any method other than GoMining does not count as a qualifying purchase.

If you haven't bought a miner, any rewards from your Bonus Miner that are over 90 days old will be deleted automatically.

## Why hasn't my task reward been credited?

If a reward hasn't arrived, here's what to check:

1. Make sure you claimed the reward while the task was still active. Once you've met all of a task's requirements, open the task card and press **Claim** yourself. **Claim** is only available while the task is active and within its time limit; if you don't claim before it expires, the reward is lost.
2. Check the task's status in the **Not Active** tab:
   - `Completed` — you claimed in time, and the reward has been credited.
   - `Expired` — the time limit passed before you claimed, so the reward can no longer be issued.
3. Still don't see a reward for a `Completed` task? Double-check that you met all task requirements, then contact our support team at support@gomining.com.

> **These "tasks" are Bonus Miner tasks — not the Bounty Program.** When a user says a **task in the app** isn't completing/crediting (e.g. *"the task for reinvesting into GOMINING tokens is still not complete but I got confirmation"*), that is a **Bonus Miner task**, not the separate points-based **Bounty Program**. Don't route in-app "task" + "reinvest"/"tokens"/"upgrade my miner" queries to the Bounty Program. Troubleshoot as a Bonus Miner task with the steps above, plus these task-specific checks.

### The "reinvest into GOMINING tokens" task won't complete even though I set up reinvestment

For a reinvestment task specifically, check that:

- Your **Reinvestment is set to GOMINING tokens**, not to TH/hashrate. The task credits only when reinvestment is configured to the **GOMINING token** option — reinvesting into TH does not count toward it.
- You pressed **Claim** on the task card once the requirement was met (see step 1 above) — most tasks are not credited automatically.

If reinvestment is correctly set to GOMINING tokens, you got confirmation, and the task still shows incomplete after claiming, contact support at support@gomining.com so they can review it.

## What happens if I am inactive?

If you stop using the service for a long time, some of your older Bonus Miner rewards might expire. Don't worry, your Bonus Miner and any upgrades you've earned will still be on your account.

---

## How do I customize my Bonus Miner's appearance?

You can make your Bonus Miner unique by unlocking and using new colors and styles.

1. To start customizing, click the **Customize** button below your Bonus Miner.
2. From there, you can view all available customization options and apply any you've unlocked.

Some styles require completing special tasks before you can use them. If you see a lock icon, it means you need to finish a task to unlock that look.

## How does the bonus miner work in Miner Wars mode?

If you own at least one digital miner, your Bonus Miner adds its power to yours in Miner Wars.

- Your Bonus Miner earns points every second based on its power. The more power it has, the higher your chance of winning a round.
- When calculating clan rewards, your Bonus Miner's power only counts for the rounds when it was active.

## How does the bonus miner affect maintenance fees and discounts?

Once you own a digital miner, your Bonus Miner is included in all reward and maintenance fee calculations.

- All standard discounts, including VIP levels and the Service Button, apply to your Bonus Miner.
- Fees for the Bonus Miner are calculated using the same rules as your other miners.

> The Auto-reinvest in TH feature cannot be enabled for the Bonus Miner.

## What does the Bonus Miner page look like?

Open the Bonus Miner from the Miners list (My miners → Mining farm) — it is tagged **Bonus miner** and has its own ID. The page shows its current Power (in TH) and Efficiency (in W/TH), a **Customize** button, and four tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
|-----|---------------|
| **Tasks** | Available tasks, grouped by **Level** (Level 1, 2, 3 …; higher levels can be locked). A toggle switches between **Active** and **Not active** (completed/expired) tasks. Each task lists its hashrate reward. |
| **Appearance** | The cosmetic items currently equipped on the miner, grouped by slot. Prompts you to complete tasks to unlock new items. |
| **Upgrades** | History of upgrades applied to the miner (e.g. Power, Miner activation) with the power/efficiency each one added. |
| **Properties** | The description — *"Your free miner that mines Bitcoin for you. Complete tasks to upgrade it permanently and earn even more BTC"* — and a **How it works** button. |

## What can I customize on the Bonus Miner?

The **Customize** button opens a picker with one tab per part of the miner:

**Wall, Body, Bottom, Fans, Screen, Buttons, Top, Stickers.**

Each tab has a grid of items. Locked items show a lock icon; opening one shows its unlock requirement. Items can be unlocked by:

- Completing specific tasks, or
- Reaching a given **VIP level** (for example, *"Reach VIP level Bronze II"* or *"Reach VIP level Legend I"*). VIP-gated items show an **Explore VIP** link.
