# Identity Verification (KYC)

### What is KYC?

KYC stands for **Know Your Customer**. It's a standard procedure used by banks, exchanges, and any companies working with private money to verify a customer's identity and the legitimacy of their activity.

Before a financial transaction can be processed, KYC checks three key points:

1. **Identity** — who is making the transaction?
2. **Legality** — on what basis is the transaction carried out?
3. **Purpose and risks** — what is the nature of the transaction and what risks may be involved?

KYC is considered complete only when the organization receives clear answers to all three questions.

---

### What is the main purpose of KYC?

First of all, KYC is aimed at protecting financial institutions from illegal and fraudulent use of the services they provide. In particular, this applies to money laundering and terrorist financing.

KYC ensures that sufficient information about the client is received, which helps to make an objective decision about a problem-free cooperation.

---

### Why do I need to go through KYC?

GoMining holders must go through KYC to protect their addresses and transactions from illegal and fraudulent activities.

Also, identity verification is required for several actions in your GoMining personal account:

* Withdraw GOMINING tokens
* Unlock more BTC withdrawal options
* Mint, sell, and withdraw miners
* Access all GoMining Launchpad projects
* Issue a GoMining Visa card (requires KYC Advanced)

## KYC levels: KYC Basic vs KYC Advanced

> **CRITICAL: Always identify which KYC level the question is about before answering.**
> The two levels are **KYC Basic** and **KYC Advanced**. They were renamed from
> **KYC Level 1** and **KYC Level 2**; users and older help articles still use the old names.
> Read "Level 1" as KYC Basic and "Level 2" as KYC Advanced, and answer in the new vocabulary.
>
> KYC Basic and KYC Advanced have **different** document and country rules. Never apply KYC Advanced
> restrictions to a KYC Basic question, and vice versa. If the user asks about KYC Basic, answer
> with KYC Basic policy only; if they ask about KYC Advanced, apply the additional KYC Advanced rules.
> If the level is unclear, treat a general account-verification question as **KYC Basic**
> (the level needed to withdraw tokens, mint/sell miners, and access Launchpad), and mention
> that the GoMining Visa card additionally requires KYC Advanced.

| | **KYC Basic** | **KYC Advanced** |
|---|---|---|
| **What it checks** | Identity only (document + selfie) | Identity **plus** additional compliance checks |
| **Additional checks** | None | Email, phone, tax information, proof of address, financial questionnaire |
| **Accepted ID documents** | National passport, national ID, residence card, **driver's license** | National passport, national ID (varies by country); **driver's license NOT accepted** |
| **Required for** | Withdraw GOMINING tokens, unlock more BTC withdrawal options, mint/sell/withdraw miners, access all Launchpad projects | Issuing a GoMining Visa card |
| **Availability** | All countries **except prohibited jurisdictions** | Depends on country-specific compliance requirements |

## KYC Basic

KYC Basic verifies user identity through **document submission and selfie verification**.

### Accepted document types (KYC Basic)

Any **one** of the following is accepted:

* Valid national passport
* National ID
* Residence card
* Driver's license

> A passport is **not** mandatory for KYC Basic — a national ID, residence card, or driver's
> license is equally valid. Do **not** tell users they cannot complete KYC without a passport,
> and do **not** claim any specific country (e.g., Australia) is "passport-only" for KYC Basic.
> Only state that a passport is required if official policy explicitly specifies it for a
> particular verification scenario.
>
> A **US state ID or US driver's license** is a government-issued ID and **is accepted for KYC Basic**. Answer this directly — do not assert that national IDs are accepted and then hedge that you're "unsure about a US state ID specifically"; that contradicts itself.
>
> **Activation timing of a newly-issued or temporary ID is not documented.** If a user asks whether a brand-new / just-issued / temporary or paper state ID can be used **on day one** or if there is an "activation time" before it works for KYC, you do **not** have verified information on that — do not guess a waiting period or say it works immediately. direct them to GoMining support.

### Availability (KYC Basic)

KYC Basic is available in **all countries except prohibited jurisdictions**.

**Prohibited jurisdictions for KYC Basic:**

* Afghanistan
* Belarus
* Cuba
* Iran
* Myanmar
* North Korea
* Russia
* Somalia
* South Sudan
* Syria
* Venezuela
* Yemen

> **Russia and Belarus — KYC Basic will not be confirmed.** Users in **Russia or Belarus** cannot complete **KYC Basic** verification. Tell such users plainly that their KYC cannot be confirmed, rather than walking them through the steps as if it will succeed.

### How to pass KYC Basic

1. Go to **Profile** → **Security**
2. Select the **KYC** option
3. Click **Pass KYC**
4. Prepare your documents
5. Click **Start verification**
6. Select the country that issued your identity document and the type of document
7. Upload a live photo of your identity document. It should be bright and clear, with all corners of the document visible.
8. Take a selfie turning your head in a circle
9. Check the provided information.

The document should be reviewed within 3–5 minutes, but in some cases can take up to 48 hours. The verification status will be updated automatically, and we'll notify you via email.

## KYC Advanced

KYC Advanced includes everything in KYC Basic **plus** additional compliance checks:

* Email verification
* Phone verification
* Tax information
* Proof of address
* Financial questionnaire

KYC Advanced is **required to issue a GoMining Visa card** and is available only if you're eligible.

### Accepted document types (KYC Advanced)

* Accepted documents and eligibility **depend on country-specific compliance requirements** and may vary by country.
* **A driver's license is NOT accepted** as a valid proof of identity for KYC Advanced.
* Some countries have **passport-only** restrictions for KYC Advanced — the app will only show the passport option in the document picker for those countries.

> **US residents — KYC Advanced is passport-only.** When a user selects **United States of America** in the KYC Advanced verification flow, the app **only** offers **Passport** as an accepted document. **US state IDs and US driver's licenses are NOT accepted at KYC Advanced**, even though they are accepted at KYC Basic. This is not a bug or a display glitch — it is the current policy. Do not tell a US user at KYC Advanced that state IDs or driver's licenses are accepted; that mixes up KYC Basic with KYC Advanced. If the user is applying for a **GoMining Card** (which requires KYC Advanced) and asks about accepted documents, answer plainly: for US applicants, only a valid US passport works at KYC Advanced.

### How to pass KYC Advanced

1. Go to **Profile** → **Security**
2. Select the **KYC** option
3. Under the **Advanced** section, click the **Pass KYC** button
4. Click the **Start verification** button
5. Enter your email address to receive and submit a unique verification code
6. Select your country of tax residence
7. Choose your document type (requirements may vary by country; a driver's license is not accepted)
8. Provide details regarding your employment status, source of funds, and annual income
9. Submit your mobile number to receive and confirm a security code via SMS
10. Take a selfie turning your head in a circle
11. Provide proof of address

Your proof of address must be issued within the last 3 months and clearly show your full name and your residential address.

**Accepted Proof of Address (POA) documents:**

* Government organization: statements, voter registration, tax bills
* Utility provider: utility bills, telecom bills
* Bank: bank statements, bank letters
* Mobile operator: statements and bills for mobile communications
* Other: documents from employer, rent bills, lease agreements

The document should be reviewed within 3–5 minutes, but in some cases can take up to 48 hours.

> Card-specific KYC Advanced details (regional eligibility, passport restrictions for Belarus/Russia,
> document tables by European/Global residency) are maintained in the
> [gomining-card skill](../../gomining-card/references/KYC-REQUIREMENTS.md).

## Answering guidance (do / don't)

**Do:**

* Ask or infer which KYC level applies before giving document or country rules.
* For KYC Basic, confirm that passport, national ID, residence card, **or** driver's license all work.
* For KYC Advanced, note that requirements vary by country and a driver's license is not accepted.

**Don't:**

* ❌ Say "Without a passport you cannot complete KYC" for KYC Basic — that is incorrect.
* ❌ Say "Australia only accepts passports" for KYC Basic — that is incorrect.
* ❌ Apply KYC Advanced restrictions (e.g., no driver's license, passport-only) to a KYC Basic question.
* ❌ Apply KYC Basic rules to a KYC Advanced question. For US card / KYC Advanced applicants specifically, do **not** say "US state IDs or driver's licenses are accepted" — that is a KYC Basic rule and **is wrong for KYC Advanced** (US at KYC Advanced is passport-only, see above).
