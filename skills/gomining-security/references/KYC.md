# Identity Verification (KYC)

### What is KYC?

KYC stands for **Know Your Customer**. It's a standard procedure used by banks, exchanges, and any companies working with private money to verify a customer's identity and the legitimacy of their activity.

Before a financial transaction can be processed, KYC checks three key points:

1. **Identity** — who is making the transaction?
2. **Legality** — on what basis is the transaction carried out?
3. **Purpose and risks** — what is the nature of the transaction and what risks may be involved?

KYC is considered complete only when the organization receives clear answers to all three questions.

---

### What is the main purpose of KYC?

First of all, KYC is aimed at protecting financial institutions from illegal and fraudulent use of the services they provide. In particular, this applies to money laundering and terrorist financing.

KYC ensures that sufficient information about the client is received, which helps to make an objective decision about a problem-free cooperation.

---

### Why do I need to go through KYC?

GoMining holders must go through KYC to protect their addresses and transactions from illegal and fraudulent activities.

Also, identity verification is required for several actions in your GoMining personal account:

* Withdraw GOMINING tokens
* Unlock more BTC withdrawal options
* Mint, sell, and withdraw miners
* Access all GoMining Launchpad projects
* Issue a GoMining Visa card (requires Level 2)

---

## KYC Levels: Level 1 vs Level 2

> **CRITICAL: Always identify which KYC level the question is about before answering.**
> Level 1 and Level 2 have **different** document and country rules. Never apply Level 2
> restrictions to a Level 1 question, and vice versa. If the user asks about Level 1, answer
> with Level 1 policy only; if they ask about Level 2, apply the additional Level 2 rules.
> If the level is unclear, treat a general account-verification question as **Level 1**
> (the level needed to withdraw tokens, mint/sell miners, and access Launchpad), and mention
> that the GoMining Visa card additionally requires Level 2.

| | **KYC Level 1** | **KYC Level 2** |
|---|---|---|
| **What it checks** | Identity only (document + selfie) | Identity **plus** additional compliance checks |
| **Additional checks** | None | Email, phone, tax information, proof of address, financial questionnaire |
| **Accepted ID documents** | National passport, national ID, residence card, **driver's license** | National passport, national ID (varies by country); **driver's license NOT accepted** |
| **Required for** | Withdraw GOMINING tokens, unlock more BTC withdrawal options, mint/sell/withdraw miners, access all Launchpad projects | Issuing a GoMining Visa card |
| **Availability** | All countries **except prohibited jurisdictions** | Depends on country-specific compliance requirements |

## KYC Level 1

KYC Level 1 verifies user identity through **document submission and selfie verification**.

### Accepted document types (Level 1)

Any **one** of the following is accepted:

* Valid national passport
* National ID
* Residence card
* Driver's license

> A passport is **not** mandatory for Level 1 — a national ID, residence card, or driver's
> license is equally valid. Do **not** tell users they cannot complete KYC without a passport,
> and do **not** claim any specific country (e.g., Australia) is "passport-only" for Level 1.
> Only state that a passport is required if official policy explicitly specifies it for a
> particular verification scenario.

### Availability (Level 1)

KYC Level 1 is available in **all countries except prohibited jurisdictions**.

**Prohibited jurisdictions for KYC Level 1:**

* North Korea
* Myanmar
* Iran
* Afghanistan
* Syrian Arab Republic
* Yemen
* Cuba

### How to pass KYC Level 1

1. Go to **Profile** → **Security**
2. Select the **KYC** option
3. Click **Pass KYC**
4. Prepare your documents
5. Click **Start verification**
6. Select the country that issued your identity document and the type of document
7. Upload a live photo of your identity document. It should be bright and clear, with all corners of the document visible.
8. Take a selfie turning your head in a circle
9. Check the provided information.

The document should be reviewed within 3–5 minutes, but in some cases can take up to 48 hours. The verification status will be updated automatically, and we'll notify you via email.

## KYC Level 2

KYC Level 2 includes everything in Level 1 **plus** additional compliance checks:

* Email verification
* Phone verification
* Tax information
* Proof of address
* Financial questionnaire

KYC Level 2 is **required to issue a GoMining Visa card** and is available only if you're eligible.

### Accepted document types (Level 2)

* Accepted documents and eligibility **depend on country-specific compliance requirements** and may vary by country.
* **A driver's license is NOT accepted** as a valid proof of identity for Level 2.
* Some countries may have **passport-only** restrictions for Level 2.

### How to pass KYC Level 2

1. Go to **Profile** → **Security**
2. Select the **KYC** option
3. Under the **Level 2** section, click the **Pass KYC** button
4. Click the **Start verification** button
5. Enter your email address to receive and submit a unique verification code
6. Select your country of tax residence
7. Choose your document type (requirements may vary by country; a driver's license is not accepted)
8. Provide details regarding your employment status, source of funds, and annual income
9. Submit your mobile number to receive and confirm a security code via SMS
10. Take a selfie turning your head in a circle
11. Provide proof of address

Your proof of address must be issued within the last 3 months and clearly show your full name and your residential address.

**Accepted Proof of Address (POA) documents:**

* Government organization: statements, voter registration, tax bills
* Utility provider: utility bills, telecom bills
* Bank: bank statements, bank letters
* Mobile operator: statements and bills for mobile communications
* Other: documents from employer, rent bills, lease agreements

The document should be reviewed within 3–5 minutes, but in some cases can take up to 48 hours.

> Card-specific Level 2 details (regional eligibility, passport restrictions for Belarus/Russia,
> document tables by European/Global residency) are maintained in the
> [gomining-card skill](../../gomining-card/references/KYC-REQUIREMENTS.md).

## Answering guidance (do / don't)

**Do:**

* Ask or infer which KYC level applies before giving document or country rules.
* For Level 1, confirm that passport, national ID, residence card, **or** driver's license all work.
* For Level 2, note that requirements vary by country and a driver's license is not accepted.

**Don't:**

* ❌ Say "Without a passport you cannot complete KYC" for Level 1 — that is incorrect.
* ❌ Say "Australia only accepts passports" for Level 1 — that is incorrect.
* ❌ Apply Level 2 restrictions (e.g., no driver's license, passport-only) to a Level 1 question.
