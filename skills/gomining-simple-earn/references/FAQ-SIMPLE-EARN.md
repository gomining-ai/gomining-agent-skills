# Simple Earn

### What is Simple Earn?

Simple Earn lets you earn interest on the assets you already have in your account.

Once you turn it on, your balance starts generating rewards automatically without locking your funds or limiting withdrawals.

### Who can use Simple Earn?

Simple Earn is available to GoMining users in all regions **except the United States**. US residents and citizens cannot use Simple Earn.

EEA and UK users can use Simple Earn. EEA users have a slightly different asset list — USDT is not available for EEA, but BTC, ETH, SOL, BNB, TON, and USDC are.

### How do I get started?

1. Open the **Wallet** tab.
2. Turn on the **Simple Earn** toggle.
3. Accept the **Simple Earn User Agreement**.

### Which funds earn rewards?

The supported assets depend on your region:

* **Non-EEA users:** BTC, ETH, SOL, BNB, TON, USDT, USDC
* **EEA users:** BTC, ETH, SOL, BNB, TON, USDC (USDT is not available for EEA users)

GOMINING token is excluded from Simple Earn. Specific availability may change due to local regulations.

### Is Simple Earn automatic?

Yes.

Once enabled, it runs in the background.

No need to manage positions, move funds, or manually reinvest; everything happens for you.

### What is APR?

**APR (Annual Percentage Rate)** shows how much you can earn from an asset over a year, based on current conditions.

APR is used to calculate your Simple Earn rewards.

#### Why does APR change?

APR depends on multiple factors, including:

* Market conditions
* Asset demand
* Available liquidity
* Your VIP level

That's why APR values may increase or decrease over time.

#### Why does each asset have a different APR?

Each asset has its own APR because it behaves differently in the Earn system.

For example:

* Stablecoins and volatile assets have different demand
* Liquidity conditions vary per asset
* Market activity affects rates independently

As a result, APR is calculated separately for each asset.

#### What APR values can I see?

You can see two types of rates:

* **Current APR** — the rate applied to your balance right now
* **Maximum APR** — the highest possible rate available at the Elite VIP level

Your actual APR depends on your current VIP level.

### How are rewards calculated?

Rewards are calculated based on:

* The minimum balance held during each full 4-hour cycle
* The current APR for each asset
* Your VIP Simple earn multiplier

If your balance decreases due to a withdrawal, purchase, or any other user operation, rewards are calculated based on the lowest amount held.

### How are rewards credited?

* Rewards are calculated for every full 4-hour cycle you've participated in
* **By default, rewards are paid in BTC** to your virtual wallet — added automatically even if you didn't hold BTC before
* **You can switch the reward type to TH** (computing power added to one of your miners) instead of BTC — see the *Simple Earn rewards in TH* section below

### Where can I see my earnings?

In the **Simple Earn** screen, you can track:

* Current APR
* Total BTC rewards earned
* Earning amounts per asset

All information updates automatically. The **Total rewards** figure on the Simple Earn screen is always shown in **BTC and USD**, regardless of whether individual cycles were paid in BTC or TH — the per-cycle TH amounts are visible in the reward history entries below the total.

## Simple Earn rewards in TH

Simple Earn rewards can be paid in **TH (computing power)** instead of BTC. When you choose TH, your reward is converted into computing power and added to one of your miners.

### Key facts

- **Default is BTC.** TH is an opt-in reward type — the user has to switch it on.
- **10% TH bonus.** When you choose TH, your reward comes with **10% more TH** than a straight BTC-to-TH conversion would give you.
- **Cycles still 4-hour, but TH is added once a day.** Rewards are calculated every 4-hour cycle as before, but the TH itself is added to your miner in a **single daily upgrade** that covers everything earned since the previous one.
- **TH price is live at upgrade time.** The TH price used for the daily upgrade is the price at the moment the upgrade is made — not the price at the start of the day / at each 4-hour cycle.

### How do I switch my Simple Earn rewards to TH?

On the **Simple Earn** screen:

1. Switch the **reward type** from BTC to TH.
2. Select the **miner** that will receive the TH.
3. Confirm.

From then on, your Simple Earn rewards go to the selected miner automatically. You can switch back to BTC at any time.

### Is there a minimum reward amount for TH?

**Yes — a per-cycle minimum of $0.10 USD.** TH rewards apply to a given 4-hour cycle only if that cycle's reward is **$0.10 USD or more**. If a cycle's reward is smaller than that:

- The **cycle is paid in BTC** as usual, not held / not merged with the next cycle.
- Each cycle is **checked separately**; sub-threshold cycles are **not carried over** to add up.
- Your **reward type stays set to TH** — you don't need to change anything. Whichever cycles do reach the $0.10 threshold get added to your miner in the daily upgrade; the rest are paid in BTC that day.

Whether a cycle reaches the minimum depends on your Simple Earn balance and the APR for the asset you hold. Small balances / low-APR assets may routinely fall below and land in BTC even with TH selected.

### Which miners can receive TH rewards? (eligibility)

A miner can be selected as the TH-reward destination if **all four** are true:

1. It **isn't listed for sale** on the Marketplace.
2. It has **no active MNPL** (Mine Now, Pay Later) installment plan.
3. Its energy efficiency is **≤ 20 W/TH**.
4. It **hasn't reached the maximum miner size** — there must be room to add power.

On the **Select miner** screen, every miner that doesn't qualify shows the specific **reason** next to it. If a user says a miner is greyed out or missing, look at the reason shown on that screen and address that specific blocker — do not guess.

### What if I don't have any miner?

You need a miner as the destination for the computing power. If you have none, the app offers you a path to **buy a miner starting from 1 TH**. Until you get one:

- Your reward type stays **BTC** automatically.
- Your Simple Earn balance and rewards are **not affected** — you keep earning normally, just in BTC.

### Can I split rewards between several miners?

**No.** All TH rewards go to **one selected miner**. If you own several miners you choose which one receives Simple Earn TH, and you can **change your choice at any time** — the change applies to the next reward cycle.

### What happens if I sell the miner I selected?

Your reward type **switches back to BTC automatically**, and you'll see a notification in Simple Earn asking you to select another miner. **No rewards are lost** in the switch — cycles calculated up to that point are already credited; new cycles simply go to BTC until you pick a new destination miner.

### Can I switch back to BTC?

**Yes, at any time.** Change the reward type on the Simple Earn screen; the change applies **from the next reward cycle** (in-flight cycles complete under the reward type that was set when they were calculated).

### Where can I see my TH rewards?

In your **reward history on the Simple Earn screen** — each history entry shows exactly how much TH was added for that cycle (or that cycle was paid in BTC if it was below the $0.10 threshold). The **Total rewards** figure at the top of the screen stays denominated in BTC and USD; it does not display cumulative TH separately.

### Guardrails for the assistant

- Do **not** state "Simple Earn rewards are always paid in BTC" — that's the old wording; both BTC and TH are valid reward types now.
- Do **not** invent other reward types (GMT, ETH, stablecoin) — only BTC and TH.
- Do **not** describe the switch as "changing which asset you earn on" — the choice is about how the reward is delivered, not about which asset you have deposited.
- Do **not** promise a specific TH-per-cycle number without live TH price and the user's balance/APR — apply the standard Worked-example rule with a stated assumption if a scenario answer is invited.
- For "why did I get BTC when I selected TH?" — check the $0.10 per-cycle threshold first; that's the most likely cause.
- For "my selected miner doesn't appear on the list" — the eligibility screen shows the specific reason; use the four-condition list above (not-for-sale, no MNPL, ≤ 20 W/TH, has room).

### Can I withdraw my funds?

Yes, anytime. Your money is not locked. You can withdraw your assets whenever you want, even while Simple Earn is active.

> **Simple Earn has no withdrawal speed tiers or fees.** Do **not** describe Simple Earn withdrawals using the BTC mining-reward model of a **"Fast withdrawal" (within 1 hour, network fee, KYC)** — that "Fast withdrawal" label applies only to withdrawing **BTC mining rewards** from the wallet, not to Simple Earn. Simple Earn funds are simply withdrawn from your balance at any time, with no lockup and no penalty. Never invent a "Fast withdrawal" tier for Simple Earn.

### How do I disable Simple Earn?

To disable Simple Earn:

1. Open the **Wallet** tab.
2. Select the **Simple Earn** badge.
3. Scroll down to the bottom of the page.
4. Click the **Disable Simple Earn** button.

Once you complete these steps, Simple Earn will be disabled right away.

**After disabling Simple Earn:**

* You will not earn rewards for the current 4-hour period.
* Your funds will remain fully accessible and usable.
* You can reactivate Simple Earn whenever you wish.

### I want to cancel Simple Earn and get my money back / a refund

Simple Earn isn't a purchase or a paid subscription, so there is nothing to "refund" — the balance in Simple Earn is **your own money**, and it is **never locked**. To stop and get your funds back:

1. **Disable Simple Earn** (Wallet → Simple Earn badge → **Disable Simple Earn**), and/or
2. **Withdraw your balance** at any time — there are **no lockups and no penalties**, and any rewards already credited to you are yours to keep.

Your assets simply return to being freely usable in your wallet. There is no cancellation fee and no waiting period. (Note: because rewards use the lowest balance held during a 4-hour cycle, withdrawing mid-cycle reduces that cycle's reward — but nothing is lost from your principal.)

### Are my funds safe? I'm worried about losing money

GoMining carefully selects the protocols used in Simple Earn and continuously monitors them. Protocols may be rotated if needed to reduce risks. This means the system is actively managed, not a "set and forget" solution.

At the same time, Simple Earn works with DeFi protocols, so risks cannot be fully eliminated. In rare cases, such as a smart contract vulnerability, losses may occur.

Simple Earn is not a bank deposit, and capital preservation cannot be absolutely guaranteed. To reduce risk, GoMining monitors protocols, rebalances allocations, and may limit exposure as needed.

### What do you do with my assets?

Simple Earn lets your crypto assets earn rewards while they remain in your account.

Once enabled, GoMining handles the process. The platform selects protocols, allocates funds, monitors risks, and rebalances when needed.

Rewards are paid in BTC every 4 hours, with no lockups. You can withdraw your assets at any time.

At the moment, we do not disclose real-time allocation details. We are working to provide more transparency into the strategies and mechanisms used in the future.

### Where are my funds allocated?

GoMining works with selected DeFi protocols, including staking, lending, and liquidity solutions. Protocols are chosen based on reliability, liquidity, and yield.

Allocations are continuously monitored and may be rotated or rebalanced depending on market conditions.

At this time, we do not publish a fixed list of protocols because allocations may change. Improving transparency remains one of our priorities.

The main value of Simple Earn is that users do not need to select or monitor protocols themselves. GoMining manages this process.
