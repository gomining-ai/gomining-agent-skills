# GoMining Academy — App Layout & Navigation

How the Academy appears in the app. The individual article titles and dates on the home page change over time; the structure below is the stable part. Full course lesson breakdowns are in [COURSES.md](COURSES.md).

## Getting there

Open **Academy** from the top navigation of the GoMining app. The Academy has its own top nav: **Courses**, **Articles**, **Certificates**, and a link back to **GoMining** (returns to the main app). A search is available in the top-right.

The home page header reads "GoMining Academy — Free courses, guides, and articles to kickstart your journey into crypto and mining," with a **Start exploring** button.

## Courses ↔ Bonus Miner

A course page shows the course level (e.g. Beginner), its lesson list, and progress (lessons completed, tasks, % progress) with a **Continue learning** button.

Completing course lessons ties into the **Bonus Miner**: the course page offers **Activate your bonus miner** — "Unlock the tasks and start earning rewards by completing lessons." So working through Academy lessons is one way to unlock Bonus Miner tasks and rewards (see the Bonus Miner reference in gomining-promos).
