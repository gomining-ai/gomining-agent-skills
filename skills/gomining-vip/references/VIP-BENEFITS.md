**Column notes:**

- **Discount** — maintenance discount % applied to daily electricity + service fees on the user's miners.
- **Card Cashback** — GoMining Card cashback on eligible transactions.
- **Simple Earn Multiplier** — multiplier applied to the base Simple Earn APR for this tier.
- **Travel Cashback** — cashback on GoMining Travel bookings.
- **Instant Funds Fee** — the fee for Instant Funds withdrawals; lower % = better (the fee drops as tiers rise).
- **Referral Royalty** — royalty on referrals' mining activity (progressive with tier).
- **Launchpad Allocations** — allocation multiplier and Launchpad Tier for token sales.
- **Feature** — the tier-specific perk highlighted in the All VIP levels screen. Blank in the source = no extra highlighted feature on that sub-tier.
- **GoBox** — the GoBox rewarded at this tier (collection × count).

## Two `Coming soon` Features

The source screen marks **Legend I** and **Elite** with a **"Coming soon"** feature entry. The feature itself is not yet announced — do not speculate what it will be. If a user asks, say the tier reserves a "Coming soon" perk that hasn't been detailed yet, and offer to connect them with GoMining support if they want confirmation.

## Diamond I — Reinvestment in TH (+10%)

Diamond I unlocks a **+10% bonus** on **TH reinvestment**. This is separate from any lower-tier reinvestment mechanics. Do not conflate it with the general reinvestment flow available at lower tiers — the +10% figure specifically belongs to Diamond I as its tier feature per the screenshot source.

## Platinum I — VIP-support and Special Offers

Platinum I is the entry to the paid-tier band. Its **VIP-support and special offers** feature covers the dedicated VIP manager and periodic offers targeted at the Platinum+ band. This aligns with existing docs on the [Platinum+ subscription](FAQ-VIP-SUBSCRIPTION.md), which grants Platinum I-level benefits without holding the underlying TH/veGMT.

## Bronze / Silver / Gold — Not in these screenshots

The source screenshots cover Platinum I → Elite only. For Bronze / Silver / Gold tier benefits, refer to the **All VIP levels** screen in the app (chevron-expand each level to see its full benefits list). Do not fabricate specific percentages for those tiers from memory; if a user asks for exact Bronze/Silver/Gold benefits, direct them to the in-app screen or contact GoMining support.

## Launchpad — Sub-tier detail

Launchpad allocation increases through Platinum I → III (x60 → x125 → x330), then plateaus at x700 (Tier 9) from Diamond I onward through Elite. So the biggest Launchpad step is Platinum III → Diamond I; every tier above Diamond I gives the same **x700 / Tier 9** allocation as Diamond I.

## Referral Royalty — Progressive Bands

The screenshot shows referral royalty in three flat bands across Platinum → Elite:

| Band | Royalty |
|------|---------|
| Platinum I / II / III | 12% |
| Diamond I / II / III / IV / V | 14% |
| Legend I / II / III / IV / V / Elite | 15% |

Sub-levels within a band do not change the royalty %. For lower-band rates (Bronze / Silver / Gold), refer users to the in-app **Referral mining royalty** card on the VIP status screen — do not quote a figure not in this source.

## Tier Qualification Thresholds

For per-tier thresholds (TH / veGOMINING / referral activity) see the full table in [VIP Level Thresholds](VIP-LEVEL-THRESHOLDS.md). Any single path is enough to reach a tier — you do not need all three.

## Clan Creation (Miner Wars)

- **Requirement:** Gold I VIP tier or above
- **Royalties:** 5% of GOMINING generated through Clan members' activity
- **Management:** Clan leaders can invite members, set rules, and manage Clan strategy
- **Restriction:** Clan creation is disabled if tier drops below Gold I

## Dedicated VIP Manager

Tied to the **Platinum I "VIP-support and special offers"** feature. Available from Platinum I upward (including via the Platinum+ subscription).

- **Services:** Priority support, personalized account guidance, and direct communication channel
- **Response Time:** Faster than standard support queues

## Elite tier — behaviour notes

- The Elite row's Card Cashback (5%), Travel Cashback (4%), and Referral Royalty (15%) are the same as Legend I–V.
- Elite's differentiators are the highest **Discount (6%)**, highest **Simple Earn multiplier (x1.46)**, lowest **Instant Funds Fee (1.5%)**, largest **GoBox (Legend ×6)**, and a **Coming soon** feature.
- Elite is **not** listed as invitation-only in the source screenshot; it is reached by any of the three thresholds (1M TH / 10M veGOMINING / $100M referral activity). Do not tell users it is invitation-only.
