# Card Cashback Program

### How does the GoMining Card cashback program work?

Every successful card transaction automatically earns cashback that is converted into TH (Terahash) and credited to one of your miners. Regular card usage directly increases your mining power.

### What form does cashback take?

Cashback is credited as **TH (Terahash)** added directly to a selected miner — not as BTC or tokens.

### How much cashback do I earn?

The cashback percentage depends on your GoMining VIP level. Higher VIP levels earn higher cashback rates. You can check your current cashback percentage on the GoMining VIP Program page.

### When is cashback credited?

Cashback is credited **daily** for successful card transactions:

* If a miner is selected to receive cashback, the accumulated amount is credited the **next day**.
* If no miner is selected, cashback accumulates on your cashback balance and is credited the **day after you select a miner**.

### Which miner receives the cashback?

You choose which miner receives your cashback TH. You can change your selected miner in the card settings.

### Which miners are eligible to receive cashback?

Almost any miner can receive cashback, **except** for the following ineligible categories:

| Ineligible Miner Type | Reason |
|----------------------|--------|
| Miners purchased via installments (MNPL) not yet fully paid off | Active payment plan restriction |
| Miners listed for sale on the Marketplace | Listed miners are locked from upgrades |
| Miners with efficiency less than 20 W/TH | Efficiency threshold requirement |

### What conditions must be met for cashback to be credited?

All three conditions must be met simultaneously:

1. You made a successful card transaction.
2. You have at least one eligible miner.
3. A miner is selected to receive the cashback TH.

### Does cashback require a minimum transaction amount?

No minimum transaction amount is required. Every successful transaction earns cashback.

### Do declined or refunded transactions earn cashback?

No. Only **successful** transactions earn cashback. Declined or refunded transactions do not qualify.
